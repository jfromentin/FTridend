\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
A class for levelled Schroeder forest. 

A levelled Schroeder forest with $n+1$ leaves is entierly characterized by an strictly increasing chain $(c_0,\ldots,c_h)$ of the poset $(\mathcal{P}([1..n]),\subseteq)$ stisfying $c_h=[1..n]$.
